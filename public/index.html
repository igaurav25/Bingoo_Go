How to Play

1. Arrange

Swap numbers 1-25 on your paper card or use Auto Arrange, then lock your card.

2. Cut Lines

Take turns calling numbers. Completing any row or column draws a red pen stroke and strikes letters B-I-N-G-O.

3. Claim BINGO

When all 5 letters are struck, the golden BINGO button unlocks. Tap it first to win!
